Privacy Policy

RepLedger for iPhone and iPad

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version

RepLedger does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your information. There is no account to create, no analytics, and no advertising. The app contains no networking code at all, so nothing you enter can leave your device.

What the app stores

RepLedger stores the training information you enter — workouts, the exercises in them, your sets, reps and weights, and your preferences for weight unit and appearance.

All of it is written to your device's local storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and the developer has no access to it or ability to view it.

What the app does not do

RepLedger does not:

• Collect personal information or require an account
• Use analytics, crash reporting or advertising services
• Include any third-party SDKs or trackers
• Request access to your location, contacts, photos, or Health data
• Send any data over the internet

Backups

Because your data is stored on your device, it is included in the backups you already make through iCloud Backup or your computer. Those backups are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy — the developer has no involvement in or access to them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a workout in the app removes it. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data. Since nothing is stored off your device, there is nothing else to request the deletion of.

Children

RepLedger does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes how data is handled — such as syncing between devices — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the date above will change.
